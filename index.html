What you get

The brokerage is meant to be ready when you are.

Four parts of joining Criterion. The team fills in the current details when you talk.

  1. 01

    Turnkey support

    Newer agents often spend the first stretch building a setup instead of learning the work. Criterion packages the support: onboarding, day-to-day guidance, and a team you can ask. What is in the package today is described in conversation, so this page does not invent a feature list.

  2. 02

    Company-generated leads

    The brokerage generates leads for the team. You are not handed a name and told to find every opportunity yourself. How a lead reaches you, and what is expected when it does, is part of the same conversation. Lead counts are not quoted here.

  3. 03

    A fair payout model

    You should understand how you are paid before you decide. Someone from the team walks through the model in plain language. Splits, fees, and percentages are not published on this page.

  4. 04

    Licensed in Connecticut and New York

    Criterion Real Estate LLC is licensed in both states. If you plan to practice in CT, NY, or both, you are in the right place. The area is described that way only — there is no street address on this page.

How it works

Three steps, then a real conversation.

  1. 01

    Tell us where you are

    Licensed, in a pre-licensing course, or not yet started. Add how long you have held a license, and a note if you want one.

  2. 02

    Talk with the team

    We cover the support package, how company-generated leads work, and the payout model. Bring questions. You do not have to decide on that conversation.

  3. 03

    Join if it fits

    If you and the team both want to continue, you come onto the brokerage with the support package in place. Until then, the form is only an introduction.

Who it's for

Newer agents, and people still on the way to a license.

This page is written for agents early in the work in Connecticut or New York, including people who are not licensed yet. If you have been at it longer and the support still sounds right, you can write too.

  • You hold a license and want structure while you learn the business.
  • You are in a pre-licensing course and want a brokerage in mind.
  • You have not started the course, and you want to understand the path first.
  • You would rather learn inside a team than assemble every system alone.

FAQ

Questions agents ask before they reach out.

Do I need an active license to get in touch?

No. Tell us whether you are licensed, in a pre-licensing course, or have not started yet. The team can talk with you at each of those points.

Where would I practice?

Connecticut and New York. Criterion Real Estate LLC is licensed in both states. This page does not list an office address.

Will I see the payout model before I commit?

Yes. Someone from the team explains how pay works in conversation. This page does not publish percentages, splits, or fees.

What does company-generated leads mean here?

The brokerage creates leads for the team, rather than expecting every newer agent to source all of their own. How leads are shared is discussed with you. We do not publish lead counts.

What is in the support package?

Onboarding, day-to-day guidance, and the working setup newer agents need so the business side is not left entirely to you. The team describes the current package, because it can change.

What happens after I send the form?

If you agree to be contacted, Criterion may follow up by phone, text, or email about joining the team. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. Sending the form does not obligate you to join.
